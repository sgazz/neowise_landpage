FAQ

Questions before applying.

Is the beta free?

The private beta is intended for early testing and feedback. If pricing changes later, beta participants will be informed clearly before any paid plan is introduced.

Who can apply?

Students, secondary school pupils, lifelong learners, educators, mentors, and parents interested in better learning support can apply.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We are looking for real learners, not technical testers only.

How much time does beta testing require?

You can use Neowise with your own learning goals. Some testers may be invited to short feedback conversations, but participation should remain manageable.

Is this a finished product?

No. The beta is an early version. Some features may change, improve, or occasionally fail.

Does Neowise replace teachers?

No. Neowise is designed as a personal learning layer that supports understanding, practice, memory, and progress between classes, courses, or independent study.

How will my data be handled?

The beta will follow the site's Privacy Policy. Learning and contact data should be used only to manage beta access, improve the product, and communicate about Neowise.
