FAQ

Questions before joining the beta.

Is Neowise another ChatGPT wrapper?

No. Generic AI chat answers questions in the moment. Neowise is designed around continuity: what you are learning, what you understand, what you need to practice, what you forget, and what should come next.

Who is Neowise for?

Neowise is for students, exam preparation, secondary school pupils, and lifelong learners who want more personal structure while learning.

Does Neowise replace teachers?

No. Neowise is a personal learning layer. It helps learners prepare, practice, understand, and continue between classes, courses, or independent study.

When will the beta open?

The private beta is opening gradually. Early members will be invited in small groups so we can learn from real usage and improve the product carefully.

Will my learning data be private?

Privacy is a core product requirement. The beta will be designed with clear consent, transparent data use, and GDPR-aware practices.
